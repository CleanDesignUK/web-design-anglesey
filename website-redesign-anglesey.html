Redesign audit

Warning signs your website needs attention.

A redesign should start with what is losing trust or leads, not with random visual changes.

Visitors do not enquire

People land on the site but the message, proof or form journey does not push them to act.

Mobile feels awkward

Menus, forms, spacing or content may be hard to use on phones.

Search signals are weak

Old pages, broken structure, weak headings and missing service pages reduce visibility.

Safe rebuild method

Redesign without damaging what already works.

A redesign can hurt SEO if URLs, headings, metadata and internal links are ignored. The rebuild needs a controlled plan.

Keep useful URLs

Protect pages already getting impressions and improve their purpose.

Redirect carefully

Old pages should point to the right replacement, not disappear.

Track the new journey

Measure forms, WhatsApp, email and thank-you page views after launch.
