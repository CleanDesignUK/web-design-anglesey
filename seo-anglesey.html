GEO and AEO system

AI visibility is built page by page.

The goal is not to trick AI tools. The goal is to make the business clear, useful and trustworthy enough to be understood and shortlisted.

Entity clarity

Consistent business name, areas, services, contact routes, profiles and structured data.

Answer readiness

Direct answers to buyer questions such as who you help, where you work, what changes and what happens next.

Proof and prominence

Case studies, reviews, GBP, local pages and images support trust signals across search.
