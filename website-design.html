The website job list

What your website must do

A strong local website has to explain the service in seconds, prove credibility, show the location fit and make contact easy.

Explain quickly

Visitors should understand what you offer and whether you work in their area without hunting through the page.

Build confidence

Use testimonials, examples, badges, photos and clear process to reduce hesitation.

Make action easy

Forms, WhatsApp and email need to appear at the right points in the journey.

Page flow

A page built around how customers decide

The design is planned around what a buyer needs to believe before they enquire.

Top section

Outcome, area, proof bullets and a short review form. No clutter and no forced hero image.

Middle page

Service explanation, proof, process, platforms and objections answered.

Lower page

A second form after the visitor has enough confidence to ask for help.
